# Salvor Trademark & Brand Policy

Salvor's source code and project documentation are released under the
[MIT license](./LICENSE). This policy covers something the MIT license does
not: the **Salvor name, logo, wordmark, and distinctive official brand
artwork**. It does not restrict any of the rights the MIT license grants to
the code — you may fork, modify, sell, and redistribute the code under MIT.
This policy only governs the brand identity.

## What MIT covers

The MIT license governs Salvor's source code and project documentation. Nothing
here limits those rights.

## What this policy covers

The Salvor name, logo, wordmark, and distinctive official brand artwork are the
project's brand identity. They are **not** granted for use as the brand identity
of a derivative product.

## What you may do

- Make truthful, nominative references to the project — for example,
  "compatible with Salvor", "forked from Salvor", or "uses the Salvor protocol".
- Use unmodified official screenshots or social cards when writing about,
  reviewing, or linking to the official project.

## What requires a distinct identity

- Publicly distributed forks must use a **distinct project name and visual
  identity**, unless the maintainer explicitly approves use of the official
  branding.
- No fork, service, package, marketplace listing, domain, or commercial
  offering may imply that it is the official Salvor project, that it is
  sponsored by Salvor, or that it is endorsed by Dan Wasyluk, without
  permission.

## Note

"Salvor" is not claimed here as a registered trademark. This policy governs the
name, logo, and brand identity as described above; it does not restrict the
rights granted to the code by the MIT license.

Questions about brand or name use? Open an issue or contact the maintainer.
